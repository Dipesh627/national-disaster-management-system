In short

  • We collect what is needed to run your account and handle your reports: your name, email, an optional phone number, and the details, location and photos you choose to submit.
  • Your reports and account details are visible to you and to authorized NDMS administrators. Public disaster pages never show who reported an incident.
  • The only outside service we use is Google Maps. We have no advertising or analytics trackers.
  • You can update your profile yourself and ask us about correcting or deleting your information.

This summary is only a guide. The full Policy below is what applies.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how NDMS, the National Disaster Management System, handles personal information when you visit the website, register an account, report an incident or contact support. “NDMS”, “we” and “us” mean the team that operates and administers the platform. It should be read with our Terms & Conditions.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information the platform needs in order to work. It falls into these groups, each described in the sections that follow:

  • account information you give when you register and edit your profile;
  • contact information, meaning your email address and optional phone number;
  • disaster report information, including location and photos;
  • feedback and support requests you send us;
  • basic technical information kept through cookies and browser storage.

Visitors who only read public pages do not need to give us any personal information.

3. Account Information

When you register or update your profile we store:

  • your first name, last name and username;
  • your password, stored in a scrambled (hashed) form so that it cannot be read back by us;
  • an optional profile photo;
  • your account type (citizen), whether the account is active, and the dates you joined and last signed in;
  • the date, time and version of the Terms & Privacy Policy you accepted at registration;
  • preferences you choose in Settings, such as date format, time zone and default disaster type.

4. Contact Information

We store your email address, and your phone number if you choose to add one. We use your email to identify your account, to send password reset messages, and to follow up on feedback or support requests. Your phone number is optional.

5. Disaster Report Information

When you submit a report we store:

  • the disaster type, the severity as you reported it and the time the incident began;
  • your description of what happened;
  • the incident location and address (see Section 6) and any photos (see Section 7);
  • the time you submitted the report, its review status (Pending, Under Review, Verified or Rejected), and the notifications we send you about it.

Each report is linked to your account. Reports are not published on public pages. When a report helps verify an official disaster, public pages show only the number of verified reports, not who made them.

6. Location Information

NDMS collects the location of the incident you are reporting, as exact map coordinates and an address. You choose it by picking a point on the map, searching for a place, or pressing “Use Current Location”. Only when you press that button does your browser ask permission to share your device’s position, and you can refuse. NDMS does not track your location in the background. Reported locations must be within Nepal.

The incident location may be close to where you are, so please think about that before you submit. To turn coordinates into an address and to search for places, the platform sends them to Google Maps (see Section 16).

7. Uploaded Images and Files

You can upload photos with a report (JPEG, PNG or WebP), a profile photo, and an attachment (JPEG, PNG or PDF) with a support request. Files are stored on the NDMS server exactly as you upload them.

Please note: NDMS does not remove the hidden metadata that a photo may contain, such as the time it was taken, the device used, or GPS coordinates. Consider this before uploading a photo.

Report photos are shown to you and to authorized NDMS administrators. They are made public only if an administrator selects a photo to appear in an official disaster record.

8. Feedback and Support Information

If you send feedback we store your email address, your rating and your message. If you send a support request through Help & Support we store its type, subject and description, any attachment, its status, and the reply from an administrator. This is seen by you and by authorized NDMS administrators.

9. How We Use Information

We use personal information to:

  • create and manage your account and let you sign in;
  • receive, review, verify and track disaster reports, and link verified reports to official disaster records;
  • send you notifications, such as changes to the status of your report, alerts and replies to support requests;
  • respond to feedback and support requests;
  • keep the platform secure and detect false reports, abuse and misuse;
  • understand how the platform is used and improve it, including producing summary figures about reports and disasters.

We do not sell personal information and we do not show advertising.

10. How We Protect Information

  • Passwords are stored hashed, and password rules are enforced when you register or change your password.
  • Forms are protected against cross-site request forgery, and access to citizen and administrator areas is controlled by account type.
  • Uploaded files are checked for permitted types and sizes.
  • When NDMS runs with its production security settings, connections use HTTPS and cookies are marked secure.
  • Deactivated accounts are signed out and blocked from signing in.

No website or storage system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please keep your password private and sign out on shared devices.

11. Who Can Access Information

  • You can see your own account, reports, notifications and support requests.
  • Authorized NDMS administrators can see account details (name, username, email, phone, profile photo, join date and status), reports with their locations and photos, feedback, and support requests, so they can manage the platform.
  • The technical team that maintains the platform and its database may access data as needed to keep it running.
  • The public and other citizens cannot see your account details or your reports. Emergency agencies listed in the directory do not have automatic access to NDMS accounts or reports.

12. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. We may share information only:

  • with responsible authorities or emergency agencies, when it is reasonably necessary to assess or coordinate a response to a reported incident, and limited to what is needed for that purpose;
  • with the service providers that help us run NDMS, such as Google Maps for map features, and any hosting or email service the operators use, and only so they can provide that service;
  • when the law requires it, or when it is needed to protect people’s safety or NDMS’s rights.

13. Data Retention

  • Account information is kept while your account exists. Deactivating an account does not delete it.
  • You can delete a report yourself only while it is Pending. Reports that have been processed may be kept as official records.
  • Information that an administrator has published as part of an official disaster record stays part of that record.
  • Feedback and support requests are kept as long as needed to follow up and to improve the platform.

NDMS does not currently apply an automatic deletion schedule. You can ask us to review or delete your information (see Section 14). We will consider each request, taking into account the need to keep official records and any legal requirements.

14. Your Rights and Choices

  • Correct your details: edit your name, email, phone number and profile photo in your Profile, and change your password there too.
  • Manage preferences: change your date format, time zone and default disaster type in Settings.
  • Delete a pending report: you can delete your own report while it is Pending.
  • Control your device: you can refuse or withdraw location permission in your browser or device settings, and choose which photos you upload.
  • Ask us: you can ask to access, correct or delete your information, or to close your account, by contacting us (see Section 18). There is no self-service account deletion at this time.

The law that applies to you may give you further rights, and we will respond to requests as reasonably as we can.

15. Cookies and Session Information

NDMS uses only the cookies and browser storage it needs to work. It does not use advertising or analytics cookies.

  • Session cookie: keeps you signed in. It ends when you close your browser, or lasts longer if you choose “Remember me”. It stores only a session identifier, never your password.
  • CSRF cookie: helps protect forms from being submitted by other websites.
  • Language preference: the Awareness & Guidelines page saves your language choice in your browser’s local storage. It stays on your device and is not sent to us.

Pages that show a map load Google Maps, which may use its own cookies or similar technologies as described in Google’s privacy policy. You can block or clear cookies in your browser settings, but you may not be able to sign in if you block the session cookie.

16. Third-Party Services

Google Maps. NDMS uses the Google Maps Platform (the Maps JavaScript, Geocoding and Places services) to show maps, convert between coordinates and addresses, and search for places. These appear when you report an incident, view a report, or view a disaster page. When a page with a map loads, your browser connects to Google and Google receives technical information such as your IP address. Coordinates and search text you enter on the map are also sent to Google to produce results. Google’s use of that information is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Maps Additional Terms of Service.

Email. Messages such as password resets are sent through the email service that the NDMS operators configure.

NDMS does not use third-party analytics, advertising, social-media tracking or payment services.

17. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy when NDMS changes or the law requires it. The “Last Updated” date and version at the top show the current revision. For significant changes we may also tell you through the platform, such as with a notification.

18. Contact and Support

For questions about this Policy or requests about your information, email {{ legal_contact_email }}. Signed-in citizens can also use Help & Support. NDMS is based in Dhangadhi, Nepal.
