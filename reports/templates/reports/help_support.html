THE PROCESS

How NDMS Works

01
Report

Citizens submit disaster incidents with details, location and photos.

02
Review

The submitted incident is reviewed by authorized personnel.

03
Verify

Authorized personnel verify the report and, where applicable, link it to an official disaster.

04
Inform

You're notified of the outcome, and official disaster information and alerts are published when relevant.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Go to Report Incident, select the disaster type, provide a description, select the incident location, optionally upload supporting photos, and submit the report.

The location helps NDMS identify where the incident occurred and allows responsible authorities to assess and coordinate an appropriate response.

Yes. Citizens can upload up to 5 supporting photos with an incident report.

Open My Reports from the dashboard sidebar to view and track submitted incidents and their current status.

  • Pending — the report has been submitted and is waiting for review.
  • Under Review — the report is currently being reviewed by authorized personnel.
  • Verified — the report has been verified.
  • Rejected — the report was reviewed but could not be accepted as a valid report.

Open Alerts from the dashboard sidebar to view important NDMS alerts and updates.

Notifications may be provided when there are important updates relevant to the citizen or disaster situation.

Use the support/contact information provided in the Contact Support section below.

Open My Profile from the dashboard sidebar to update your profile details, or go to Settings to change your password and manage account preferences.
