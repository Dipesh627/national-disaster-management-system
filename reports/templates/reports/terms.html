In short

  • Report only incidents you have genuinely seen or have good reason to believe are real, and describe them as accurately as you can.
  • A report is not verified automatically. Authorized NDMS administrators review it, and it may be verified or rejected.
  • NDMS is not an emergency call or dispatch service. If someone is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services first.
  • Keep your password private. False, abusive or harmful use can lead to your account being deactivated.

This summary is only a guide. The full Terms below are what apply.

In an emergency

Submitting a report on NDMS does not call for help. If there is an immediate risk to life, contact the appropriate local emergency services directly.

1. Introduction

Welcome to NDMS, the National Disaster Management System. NDMS is a web platform that lets registered citizens report disaster incidents in Nepal, share the location and details of those incidents, and follow the status of their reports. Authorized NDMS administrators review reports, manage official disaster records, publish alerts and response updates, and maintain a directory of emergency agencies. The platform also provides awareness and guidelines content, notifications, and a support and feedback channel.

In these Terms, “NDMS”, “we” and “us” mean the team that operates and administers the NDMS platform, and “you” means anyone who visits or uses it. Please read these Terms together with our Privacy Policy, which explains how personal information is handled.

2. Acceptance of Terms

By creating an NDMS account, or by using the platform, you confirm that you have read these Terms and the Privacy Policy and that you agree to be bound by them. If you do not agree, please do not register or use the features that require an account. Public pages such as Home, About, Disasters, Awareness & Guidelines and the Emergency Agencies directory can be read without an account.

3. User Account and Registration

Reporting incidents and using features such as My Reports, Profile, Settings and Help & Support requires a citizen account. To register you must provide your first name, last name, a username, an email address and a password. A phone number is optional. Passwords must meet the platform’s password rules.

  • Provide information that is accurate and keep it up to date in your Profile.
  • Use one account for yourself. Do not create accounts on behalf of others without their knowledge, or register in someone else’s name.
  • If you are under the age of majority where you live, please use NDMS together with a parent or guardian.
  • Administrator accounts are created by NDMS and are not available through public registration.

When you register, NDMS records the date and time and the version of these Terms and the Privacy Policy that you accepted (see Section 18).

4. User Responsibilities

When you use NDMS you agree to:

  • submit reports and information honestly and in good faith;
  • use the platform only for lawful purposes and in line with these Terms;
  • respect the privacy and safety of other people, including anyone who appears in a photo you upload;
  • keep your login details confidential (see Section 10);
  • follow the instructions of the responsible authorities in a real emergency, and not rely on NDMS alone for urgent help.

5. Disaster Report Submission

A disaster report can include the type of disaster, the severity as you see it, when the incident began, a description, the incident location, an address, and photos. Locations must be within Nepal. You choose the location on the map or use your device’s location when you press the “Use Current Location” button and your browser allows it.

How reports are handled:

  • A new report starts as Pending. Authorized NDMS administrators may move it to Under Review, Verified or Rejected, and you will be notified of these changes.
  • A verified report may be linked to an official disaster record. Reports may be rejected if they are incomplete, duplicated, cannot be verified, or do not meet these Terms.
  • Submitting a report does not guarantee that anyone will respond, or how quickly.
  • You can delete a report yourself only while it is still Pending. Once a report is being processed it may be kept as an official record.

6. Accuracy of Information

Report what you have actually observed, and give your best honest estimate where you are not sure. The severity and start time you enter are your own assessment. NDMS administrators may review, correct or reclassify information and may record a different severity in the official disaster record.

Information that NDMS publishes, including disaster records, alerts, response updates, guidelines and the emergency agency directory, is provided in good faith. It may be incomplete, delayed or out of date, and agency contact details can change. For decisions that affect safety, please confirm information with the responsible authorities.

7. Uploaded Content and Images

You may upload photos with a report (JPEG, PNG or WebP, up to 8 MB each and 5 per upload), a profile photo, and an attachment to a support request (JPEG, PNG or PDF, up to 5 MB). By uploading content you confirm that:

  • you took it yourself or have the right to share it;
  • it genuinely relates to the report or request you are making;
  • it is lawful, and does not contain malicious files, or unnecessarily show identifiable people, private documents or graphic material.

You keep ownership of your content. You give NDMS a non-exclusive, royalty-free permission to store it, to show it to authorized administrators, and to use it to review and respond to your report. Report photos are not made public automatically. If an administrator selects a photo to appear in an official disaster record, it may be shown publicly as part of that record. NDMS may remove content that breaks these Terms.

8. Responsible Use of the Platform

Use NDMS to report real incidents and to stay informed. Do not use it as a substitute for emergency services. For immediate danger, contact the appropriate local emergency services first, and then report on NDMS if it is safe to do so. Please submit a single report per incident where you can, add useful detail instead of repeating the same report, and use Help & Support for problems with the platform itself rather than for emergencies.

9. Prohibited Misuse

You must not:

  • submit false, misleading, exaggerated or prank reports, or reports made to cause panic or waste responders’ time;
  • impersonate another person, agency or NDMS administrator;
  • flood the platform with spam, duplicate reports or irrelevant feedback and support requests;
  • upload unlawful, abusive, hateful or graphic content, or files intended to harm a device or system;
  • share another person’s personal information without a good reason and their permission;
  • access or try to access accounts, data or admin areas that are not yours, or try to bypass or probe the platform’s security;
  • overload, disrupt or interfere with the platform, or copy its content by automated means without permission.

10. Account Security

You are responsible for what happens under your account. Choose a strong, unique password, do not share it, and sign out on shared or public devices. The “Remember me” option keeps you signed in on that device for an extended period, so use it only on devices you control. If you think someone has used your account without permission, change your password straight away (or use Forgot Password) and contact us using the details in Section 17.

11. NDMS Platform Role and Limitations

NDMS supports incident reporting, review, status tracking and public awareness. Emergency response dispatch remains coordinated with designated authorities. NDMS is not an emergency call service and does not itself send responders.

  • We do not promise that a report will be verified, acted on, or acted on within a particular time.
  • Alerts, guidelines and other content are general information and are not professional or legal advice.
  • The listing of an agency in the directory does not mean that agency endorses NDMS or has agreed to respond to reports made through it.
  • Map features are provided using Google Maps, which is a separate service (see the Privacy Policy).

The platform may be unavailable during maintenance, updates or technical problems, or when an outside service it relies on, such as Google Maps or email delivery, is not working. Features may change or be withdrawn over time. Please keep other ways of reaching help available to you.

A deactivated account cannot sign in until it is reactivated, and NDMS decides whether to do that. You may stop using NDMS at any time. To ask for your account to be closed, contact us (see Section 17). Reports that have been processed may be kept as official records, as described in the Privacy Policy.
