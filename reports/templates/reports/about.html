WHAT IS NDMS?

Built to connect information when it matters most.

The National Disaster Management System (NDMS) is a digital platform designed to make disaster information easier to report, organize, understand and follow. It gives citizens a structured way to share what they see, and gives NDMS administrators a clear process for reviewing and acting on that information.

HOW NDMS WORKS

From incident report to informed response.

NDMS follows a simple flow that helps organize disaster information from initial reporting through review and communication.

Share incident details, descriptions and photos.
