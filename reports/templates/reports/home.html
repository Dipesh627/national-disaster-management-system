How NDMS Works

From incident report to informed response.

NDMS follows a structured four-step process that organizes disaster information from initial citizen reporting through validation and coordinated response.

01 △

Report

Citizens submit an incident report with descriptions, photographs, and observed severity.

02 📍

Locate

Precise map positioning establishes exactly where the incident occurred in Nepal.

03 ✓

Verify

Submitted incident data is reviewed, structured, and organized by NDMS coordinators.

04 ⚡

Respond

Verified information supports informed response coordination and public safety updates.

ℹ NDMS supports reporting, review, status tracking, and public awareness; emergency response dispatch remains coordinated with designated authorities.
