About NDMS

One place to report, organise and follow disaster information

NDMS is a web platform for disaster reporting and public awareness in Nepal. Citizens submit incident reports, NDMS administrators review them and organise verified information into disaster records, and updates and guidance flow back to the public.

  • Structured Reporting

    Each report captures the disaster type, severity, description and photos in one consistent format that is easy to review.

  • Location-Based Information

    Incidents are recorded with a map location and address, so reviewers know exactly where to look.

  • Coordinated Information Flow

    Reviewed reports feed disaster records, response updates and notifications, so citizens and authorities work from the same information.

How NDMS works

From a citizen's report to coordinated information

Four steps keep every incident organised, located and reviewed.

  1. 01

    Report

    Submit an incident with its disaster type, severity, description and photos.

  2. 02

    Locate

    Mark where it happened on the map so the incident is placed accurately.

  3. 03

    Review / Verify

    NDMS administrators review, organise and verify the submitted information.

  4. 04

    Coordinate

    Verified information becomes disaster records and response updates that citizens can follow.

NDMS supports reporting, review, status tracking and public awareness; emergency response dispatch remains coordinated with designated authorities.
